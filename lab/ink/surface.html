Paper, ink & marker

Fibres, tooth and a faint wash sit under the page; type is laid down in ballpoint; fills in charts and illustrations take Copic-style marker, with overlapping passes, a darker wet edge and a little bleed into the sheet.

Body copy

Ballpoint ink sits unevenly on paper, pooling at the stroke edges and feathering a hair beyond them. It is oil based and slow, so it dries before it travels far, and the tooth of the paper decides where it catches. At small sizes the type stays crisp: 12px captions and 17px body remain easy to read.

Caption at 12px: the effect scales down with type size so tiny text keeps its shape.
